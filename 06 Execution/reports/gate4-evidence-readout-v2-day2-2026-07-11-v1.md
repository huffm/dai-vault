## 3. verdict

`conclusionsAllowed = false`, failing `[discrimination_inverted,
insufficient_market_disagreement]`. Coverage met (0.6723). This is the **fourth consecutive
readout** with the inversion failing, and it deepened again: −0.1184 → −0.1343 → −0.1486. The
0.80-confidence row this cohort (823604) lost, pulling `gte_0.80` from 0.4706 to 0.4444 while the
band beneath it stayed near 0.59.

The cadence produced exactly the result the 07-07 projection predicted: **discrimination is not
volume-purchasable.** Sixteen captured rows across two days (fifteen after the 823357 exclusion)
clustered at 0.75/0.80 and moved coverage, not order. The higher-confidence band remains weaker
than the one below it. This is an honest calibration result and the gate is failing correctly,
not stuck -- the criterion would report true if genuine discrimination appeared.
